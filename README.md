# ClearSheet

Business documents in. Clean, review-ready Excel out.

ClearSheet is validating three small, fixed-scope starter services:

- invoices and bills to structured Excel;
- purchase orders and supplier quotes to comparison-ready Excel;
- freight and logistics documents to a consistent shipment workbook.

## Privacy boundary

Do not upload or post real documents, customer names, addresses, payment or identity data, shipment-party data, credentials, or proprietary business values. The compatibility check runs in the browser and stores or sends nothing. The public inquiry form accepts document structure only.

All examples are labeled **DEMO / SYNTHETIC** and are not customer results or accuracy claims.
